# Quick start

1. Upload a clear, front-facing drawing, or choose **Use canvas drawing**.
2. For photographs of shaded paper, enable **Photo cleanup (line drawings)** before importing.
3. Review the suggested feature labels. Assign at least a left eye, right eye, and mouth.
4. Label pupil pieces separately to improve gaze and blinking. Assign all pieces belonging to a feature to the same label.
5. Keep **Natural mouth & eye motion** and **Keep my drawn shapes** enabled to retain the drawing’s character.
6. Choose **Build speaking face**, test the sliders, and try **Speak**.
7. Export before refreshing. Studio edits are stored in memory and are lost when the page reloads.
